About

Finance-trained, data-obsessed, and happiest somewhere between a SQL query and a pitch deck.

I lead investor-facing data work at HundredX, where I build the analytics and LLM workflows that turn tens of millions of customer feedback surveys into signals investors actually use. Before that, I spent two years in credit card and payments analytics at Argus (Verisk Financial, now TransUnion), advising some of the largest retail banks in the country.

Outside the day job I build things. I’ve founded two products, I advise early-stage founders on strategy and fundraising, and I write a lot of Python with AI tools on the side. I’m based in the New York City area. I studied finance at San Diego State, graduated magna cum laude, and still think a well-built model is a thing of beauty.
